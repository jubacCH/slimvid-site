Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026
Short version: Slimvid does not collect your personal data, photos, or videos — everything happens on your device. The only thing that leaves your iPhone is anonymous crash reports, so we can fix bugs. We don't track you or show ads.

What data we collect

Slimvid has no accounts and no servers of its own. We never collect your personal information, photos, videos, contacts, or location. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous crash and diagnostic reports (see “Crash reporting” below), used purely to fix bugs.

Your photos and videos

Slimvid needs access to your photo library so it can compress videos and find duplicate photos. All of this processing happens entirely on your device. Your media is never uploaded anywhere. You confirm every deletion, and removed items remain in your system's “Recently Deleted” album for 30 days.

Purchases

Optional Slimvid Pro purchases are handled by Apple and processed through RevenueCat, which manages subscription status. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app-user identifier and purchase information needed to validate your subscription. It does not receive your name, email, photos, or videos. See Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy policies for details.

Crash reporting

To find and fix bugs, Slimvid uses Sentry to collect anonymous crash and error diagnostics — for example the type of error, your device model, and the iOS version. These reports contain no personal information and none of your photos or videos, and the data is processed on EU servers. They are not used for advertising or tracking.

Third parties

Apart from purchase processing (RevenueCat) and crash diagnostics (Sentry) described above, Slimvid integrates no advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children

Slimvid does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the “Last updated” date.
